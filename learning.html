Everyday ways to connect

A kind welcome, a thoughtful question, or a shared project can help people feel included. Communities grow when people listen, offer support, and make space for different perspectives.

  • Notice and include someone who is new.
  • Share appreciation for a person who helped you learn.
  • Ask a trusted adult about ways to take part locally.
